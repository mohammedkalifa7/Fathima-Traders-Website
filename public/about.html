About Fathima Traders

Agricultural trade, made straightforward.

A practical B2B approach

Fathima Traders is positioned around sourcing and trading agricultural commodities, spices, pulses, oilseeds and related produce. We aim to make the buying process clear: understand the requirement, discuss commercial terms, and coordinate the next steps.

Product availability, grades, packing, quantities, delivery arrangements and prices can vary by season and market conditions. Buyers can use the enquiry form or WhatsApp to start a conversation.
